# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineering leads, CTOs, founders, enterprise operators, and automation architects managing mission-critical software assets, automation pipelines, and enterprise AI integrations.

## Product Purpose

CELAEST designs, implements, and scales enterprise-grade AI infrastructure, digital assets, and automated workflows. It provides a single centralized control center for managing licenses, team members, automated pipelines, and verified digital tools with zero friction and enterprise security.

## Positioning

Luxury enterprise AI mesh and asset infrastructure. Unlike generic SaaS marketplaces or cluttered template directories, CELAEST combines high-performance interactive micro-UIs, deterministic automation workflows, and high-security digital asset distribution within a unified obsidian aesthetic.

## Operating Context

- Modern desktop and responsive mobile web application.
- High-performance dark mode ("Obsidian Enterprise"): deep obsidian surfaces (`#030307`, `#0c0d15`), subtle violet and emerald accents, sub-pixel typography, and 120fps hardware-accelerated canvas/SVG interactions.
- Stack: Next.js 16 (Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, next-intl (ES/EN), Framer Motion, Three.js, Lucide icons.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **01 Centralized Management**: Role-based access control (Owner, Admin, Member), active session tracking, member invitations, and license management.
- **02 Automation Pipeline**: Visual workflow graph editor connecting event triggers (e.g. user creation, webhooks), execution actions (notifications, data pipelines), and automated branching logic (success/retry paths).
- **03 Asset Ecosystem**: Curated library of verified enterprise macros, Python/Node scripts, integrations (Google Sheets, Slack, CRM), and developer tools with versioned downloads.
- **Anti-Pattern Constraints**: Zero AI-clichés (no thick `border-l-2`, no meaningless gradient text on small labels, no generic placeholder data, no nested cards, no low-contrast text).

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone**: Authoritative, sleek, precise, enterprise-grade, confident.
- **Palette**: Deep Obsidian (`#030307`), Midnight Glass (`#0c0d15`), Violet Core (`#8b5cf6`, `#7c3aed`), Emerald Pulse (`#10b981`), Rose Status (`#f43f5e`), Muted Slate (`#94a3b8`).
- **Typography**: Inter / Space Grotesk with high-contrast hierarchy, light-to-bold pairings, and spacious letter-spacing on tracked labels.

## Product Principles

1. **Substance Over Decoration**: Every card represents a functional, realistic piece of software rather than illustrative fluff.
2. **Precision & Quiet Authority**: Subtle 1px bevel highlights and soft offset shadows instead of harsh glow halos or loud borders.
3. **Zero Compromise Responsiveness**: Seamless scaling from 1600px desktop monitors down to 390px mobile screens without label clipping or horizontal overflow.
