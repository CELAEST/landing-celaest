# CELAEST Landing - Obsidian & Royal Violet Palette Adaptation Standard

## 1. Palette Specifications
- **Background Root / Deep Obsidian**: `#030307` (`brand.dark`), `#020206` (`brand.deep`), `#07060e` / `#080710` (`brand.surface`), `#07060f` (containers/cards).
- **Primary Hero Violet**: `#a78bfa` (`brand.neon`, `brand.mint`).
- **Secondary Violet & Beacon**: `#8b5cf6` (`brand.neon-blue`, `brand.mint-dark`), `#c084fc` (`brand.emerald`, `brand.mint-light`).
- **Text & Accents**: `#f8f7fb` (`brand.ice`), `#ebe7f5` (`brand.soft`), `#9b95b0` (`brand.slate-light`).

- `FAQSection`: SupportNodeIcon radar/lines, interactive accordion, and bottom CTA card unified with royal violet spotlights.
- `Footer`: Ambient glows (`bg-violet-600/20`, `bg-purple-700/20`) and Logo brand glow unified.
