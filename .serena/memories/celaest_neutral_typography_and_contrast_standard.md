# CELAEST Typography & Neutral Base Standard (Restoration)

## Contrast & Typography Rules
- **Headings (Part 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` (`text-white`).
- **Headings (Part 2 / Accent)**: Clean titanium slate `#888888` (`brand.slate-light`). Zero purple tints on text.
- **Subtitles & Descriptions**: Clean neutral slate `#71717a` / `#a1a1aa` (`brand.muted`, `brand.accent`).
- **Backgrounds**: Deep neutral dark `#050505` (`brand.dark`), `#020202` (`brand.deep`), `#09090b` (`brand.surface`), `#080808` (`brand.surface-alt`).
- **Neon / Accent Glows**: Kept exclusively for active interaction highlights and beacons (`#a78bfa`, `#8b5cf6`, `#c084fc`).
